# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are everyday storytellers: people with a personal story, gift idea, family memory, or imagined book who want a complete manuscript without needing professional writing experience. Independent authors and small publishing teams are valid secondary users, but the product should remain intelligible to someone beginning with only an idea.

## Product Purpose

sopher.ai turns a short author brief into a complete, edited, exportable manuscript. Success means helping someone move from an idea to a coherent book while keeping the process understandable, interruptible, and editable.

## Positioning

sopher.ai is a complete book-writing workflow rather than a chatbot or one-shot text generator. Specialized stages develop the concept, create the outline, draft chapters in parallel, edit the prose, and check continuity across the manuscript. The author can inspect the work, approve or revise the outline, edit chapters, accept or reject suggestions, and export the result.

## Operating Context

Authors move through a library of book projects, a four-step setup flow, a project lifecycle from brief through manuscript, a live generation view, an editorial workbench, and a reading/export view. Credits are quoted before generation and metered as work runs. The interface is used on desktop, tablet, and phone; full manuscript editing is a required mobile capability.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing public routes, authenticated routes, API and Workflow-event compatibility, billing behavior, permissions, exports, and TipTap document model. Reliability data evolves only through forward migrations.
- Preserve the five public pipeline stages: Concept, Outline, Chapters, Editor, and Continuity.
- Preserve current pricing, credit-pack values, ownership language, functional claims, private-route behavior, and legal meaning.
- Marketing hierarchy and copy may be rewritten around verified facts.
- Both light and dark themes are required.
- Product costs must be presented primarily in credits; raw metered USD is secondary.
- Public pages must remain server-rendered and search-readable.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the name `sopher.ai`, the `https://sopher.ai` domain, and `support@sopher.ai`. The product voice should be clear, encouraging, literate, and direct without hype, gamification, or technical intimidation. The existing visual identity is not binding and is being replaced.

The replacement identity is dark-first, modern, and futuristic. It should make an amateur author feel that the world’s best technology is working on their behalf while remaining calm and comprehensible. Avoid bright multi-primary palettes and playful civic color blocking.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains real pipeline behavior, current pricing constants, three labeled sample openings, genre and guide content, legal drafts, and end-to-end product tests. These may demonstrate current capabilities. Do not publish named or anonymized production-run metrics, unpublished manuscript details, testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or publishing outcomes. Keep sample material clearly identifiable as sample content.

## Product Principles

1. Make the path from brief to manuscript visible.
2. Give non-expert storytellers confidence without hiding author control.
3. Prove capabilities through the working product and honest samples, not unsupported claims.
4. Keep costs, progress, ownership, and system state explicit.
5. Treat reading, writing, and revision as distinct modes with interfaces suited to each.
6. Derive one truthful next action from durable authoring evidence; never ask an author to purchase their way out of a product failure.
7. Preserve ambiguous or interrupted work until Workflow evidence makes recovery, cancellation, or failure conclusive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA across light and dark themes. Support keyboard-only use, screen readers, reduced motion, forced colors, 200–400% zoom and reflow, visible focus, and touch-friendly controls. No page-level horizontal overflow is acceptable at supported mobile widths; full manuscript editing must work on phone and tablet.
